Enrollment headcount analysis

Degree-seeking headcount over time for CS, CIS A.A.S., CNT, and department aggregate — from your app database or synced extracts.

Scope

 

Metric: headcount enrollment (majors / degree students as defined in source extracts).

Coverage: four enrollment series—program-level CS, CIS A.A.S., CNT, and CIS department aggregate—aligned to the years available in the underlying CSV / DepartmentMetric table.

Method & refresh

Figures load from the ORM when present; otherwise from static CSV under client/css/csv/. Rebuild the table after edits:

python manage.py sync_department_metrics

For filters, drill-downs, and the latest official term:  BMCC fact sheets · OIEA Tableau

Quick read — what the series suggest

  • Computer Science (A.S.) shows strong long-run growth from 2015 with more recent Fall terms (2024–2025) available in the sync; use the chart axis for exact breakpoints.
  • CIS department aggregate enrollment reflects the full CIS portfolio; compare its slope to individual majors to see concentration vs. breadth.
  • CIS (A.A.S.) and CNT are smaller programs—short-term volatility is normal at modest N; interpret changes alongside department totals.
  • Gaps on the year axis (e.g. missing Fall 2023 in some public extracts) appear as uneven spacing; that is a data availability feature, not a plotting error.
