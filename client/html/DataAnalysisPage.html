BMCC · CIS & data programs

Enrollment, completions & student composition

Trends for Computer Science, CIS, Computer Network Technology, and Data Science (A.S.), plus OIRA-based views of who studies in these programs—use alongside your advisor and official BMCC dashboards.

What you’re looking at

Enrollment: how many students were in each program over time (by year on the chart).

Degrees awarded: how many students completed each program in a given year, where we have that history (Data Science will show up when that series is included).

Composition charts: mixes of majors or race/ethnicity—based on published BMCC institutional research, with the year called out on each chart.

Quick read

  • Data Science (A.S.) (Mathematics Department, DSM-AS) is included in enrollment trends; confirm your catalog year with DegreeWorks and an advisor.
  • Computer Science shows long-run enrollment growth; use the axis for exact year labels.
  • CIS department totals help you compare breadth across the whole unit vs. individual majors.
  • Composition charts show who is in these programs (race/ethnicity from published fact sheets). Data Science (A.S.) does not yet appear in the Fall 2022 major-mix vintage; the Computer Science (Spring 2023) ethnicity chart is a reasonable peer reference for a large STEM cohort until OIRA publishes program-specific DS rows.
  • Degrees awarded lags enrollment; the mix pie uses 2022 completions from this project’s graduation files.
  • Uneven spacing on the year axis reflects missing years in the source files, not a chart bug.
