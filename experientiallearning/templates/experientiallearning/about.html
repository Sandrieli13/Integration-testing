Career readiness hub

About this project

A student-built BMCConnect space for career exploration, events, and tools that make BMCC resources easier to find.

Our mission

Welcome to our online platform for BMCC students on their career readiness journey. We focus on the challenge of opportunities going unnoticed when students do not know where to look—so we bring structured paths to jobs, internships, workshops, and community resources in one place.

Using data-informed design, we aim to guide you through options that fit your goals: interviews, skill-building, and BMCC-specific support. In the future we plan to strengthen the advisory chatbot so it can feel more like a personalized mentor for skills and next steps.
