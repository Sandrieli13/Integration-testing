The Problem

BMCC students struggle to find campus resources

The official BMCC website scatters clubs, mentors, career info, and course data across many disconnected pages — making it hard for students to navigate, especially newcomers.

⚠️ Difficult to discover & join clubs
⚠️ No centralized mentor matching system
⚠️ Career path clarity is limited
⚠️ No integrated course planning tool

Feature Deep Dive

💼 Career Development

A 3-step pipeline that takes students from major selection all the way to a matched internship.

01

🗺️ Explore Majors & Jobs

  • Pick any BMCC major (CS, CIS, Data Science…)
  • See every aligned career path & job title
  • View salary ranges powered by BLS OEWS data
  • Browse required skills for each role
02

🔍 Skill Gap Analysis

  • Your major skills vs. the job's required skills
  • Color-coded matched & missing skills list
  • Gap-filler tips: courses, labs, micro-credentials
  • Personalized advice per missing skill
03

🎯 Internship Matcher

  • Select skills & courses you've completed
  • Algorithm scores all internships for best fit
  • Autocomplete search for specific opportunities
  • One-click to view matched internship details
